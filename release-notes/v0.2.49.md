# v0.2.49

## Expert question routing

- Expert Mode now uses ordinary conversation for open-ended questions such as product descriptions, URLs, pasted material, and special context.
- Decision and confirmation questions use AskUserQuestion only when they can be represented by 2-4 clear choices.
- A rejected question card that lacks choices now tells the model how to retry a decision card or switch a genuine open question to normal text.

## Pack delivery

- Rebuilt the bundled commercialization-research-report ZIP from the latest Expert source, including the updated question-routing guidance.
