# v0.2.52

## Workflow pack upgrade reliability

- The desktop app now recognizes the exact, unchanged official v10 guided-development workflow pack and upgrades it to the bundled current pack on startup.
- User-modified workflows remain protected: an existing pack is replaced only when its workflow ID, version, and full canonical content fingerprint exactly match a recorded official default.
- Existing v10 users therefore receive the current Stage 4 host-managed batch-concurrency contract after restarting the app and starting a new workflow session.

## Workflow test alignment

- Updated workflow registry and transition tests to assert the currently shipped host-managed Stage 4 concurrency contract and normal user-confirmed completion path.
