- Business workflow questions no longer inject the Claude runtime permission selector based on words such as "authorization" or "permission". Answers such as school or data authorization now submit normally to the workflow.

- The guided development workflow can schedule independent implementation batches through the host runtime. Each batch remains sequential: Coder, then Reviewer, then any same-batch repair and re-review.
